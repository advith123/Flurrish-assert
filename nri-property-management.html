Your Chennai property. Managed from anywhere.

You may live thousands of kilometres away in the USA, UK, UAE or Singapore. Your property doesn't have to feel that way.

In short

NRI property management means a local team looks after your Chennai property while you live abroad. Flourrish finds and verifies tenants, collects rent into your Indian account, handles repairs only after you approve them, pays property tax and bills, and sends a monthly report with photos and video. The fee is 12% of the annual rent.

Ask on WhatsApp
Do you provide video reports?

Yes. Periodic video walkthroughs show the condition of your property, any maintenance issues and relevant updates, so you can see it for yourself wherever you are.

Can you manage my property if I live abroad?

Yes — NRI owners are a core focus. We communicate on WhatsApp and email at times that suit your time zone, and we can coordinate with qualified CA, legal and banking professionals for NRI-specific matters.

Can I approve major repairs?

Always. Major repairs, large expenses, new tenants, rent changes, lease renewals and renovations all require your approval. We handle the routine work; you keep control of the decisions that matter.

Do you help with property tax?

We can handle authorized property tax payments on your behalf and keep the receipts in your documents. Government charges are passed through at cost.

Do you help with utility bills?

Yes. Electricity, water, gas and apartment association dues can be paid on your authorization and reported each month, so nothing lapses while you are away.

Can you coordinate POA documentation?

We can coordinate with lawyers on Power of Attorney and other property-related documentation. Flourrish does not provide legal advice; the documentation itself is prepared and advised on by a qualified legal professional.

Do you provide tax advice?

No. Flourrish coordinates with an empanelled Chartered Accountant for matters such as TDS on rent paid to NRIs, GST, FEMA and repatriation. Professional advice should be obtained from the appropriate licensed advisor.

What is NRI property management?

NRI property management is a service where a local team looks after a property in India on behalf of an owner who lives abroad. In Chennai, Flourrish finds and verifies tenants, collects rent, inspects the property, arranges repairs with the owner's approval, pays property tax and bills, and sends a monthly report with photos and video.

Can an NRI rent out a property in India?

Yes. NRIs can rent out property they own in India. The rent is usually paid into the owner's NRO account, the tenant is generally required to deduct tax at source (TDS), and the rental income is taxable in India. A Chartered Accountant can confirm what applies to you.

How often will I get updates about my property?

You get a monthly report covering rent, tenant, bills and the property's condition, with photos and video walkthroughs. Anything urgent, such as a leak or a repair that needs your approval, is sent to you on WhatsApp as it happens.

Is it safe to rent out my Chennai flat while I live abroad?

It can be, if the basics are done properly: a verified tenant, a written and registered rent agreement, a move-in inventory with photos, regular inspections and someone local who follows up on rent and repairs. Most problems owners face from abroad come from skipping one of these steps.
