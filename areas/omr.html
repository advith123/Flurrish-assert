Property management on OMR, Chennai.

Apartments along Chennai's IT corridor, looked after by a local team, whether you live in Perungudi or Pennsylvania.

In short

Flourrish manages apartments and homes along OMR (Old Mahabalipuram Road, officially Rajiv Gandhi Salai), including Perungudi, Thoraipakkam and Sholinganallur. We find and verify tenants, collect rent, handle association dues and repairs with your approval, and send a monthly video report. The fee is 12% of the annual rent.

Local knowledge

Why owners in OMR use a property manager.

OMR is Chennai's IT corridor. Much of the housing is in gated apartment communities, and many tenants are working professionals and young families who move when their jobs do.

That means steady demand, but also more frequent tenant changes. Each changeover needs a clean handover: inspection photos, deposit settlement, meter readings, repainting if needed, and a new verified tenant as quickly as possible.

Gated communities add their own layer of admin: association maintenance charges, move-in approvals, parking stickers and building rules. We deal with the association so you don't have to.

Neighbourhoods

Where we work in OMR.

Perungudi

Close to the northern end of OMR and the older IT parks. Mostly apartments, with steady demand from people working nearby.

Thoraipakkam

Dense residential stretch between Perungudi and Sholinganallur, with a mix of apartment blocks and independent houses.

Sholinganallur

The junction where OMR meets the link road to ECR. Large gated communities and a busy rental market.

Further along OMR

Navalur, Siruseri, Kelambakkam and beyond. Message us with the address and we'll confirm whether we can cover it.

Do you manage flats in gated communities on OMR?

Yes. Most of the OMR properties we look after are in gated communities. We handle the association side too: maintenance charges, move-in and move-out approvals, and building rules.

Who pays the association maintenance charges when the flat is rented?

It depends on your rent agreement. Some owners include it in the rent, others ask the tenant to pay it directly. We write it into the agreement and check every month that it has been paid.

What happens when my OMR tenant gives notice?

We inspect the flat with photos during the notice period, agree any deposit deductions with you, arrange repairs or repainting if needed, and start looking for the next verified tenant immediately.

Do you cover Navalur, Siruseri and Kelambakkam?

Our primary focus is Perungudi to Sholinganallur. For homes further along OMR, send us the address on WhatsApp and we'll confirm.
