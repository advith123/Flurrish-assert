Local knowledge

Why owners in Tambaram and GST Road use a property manager.

GST Road (Grand Southern Trunk Road) runs south from the city past the airport towards Chengalpattu. The suburbs along it have grown quickly, with new apartment projects, independent houses and plotted developments.

Many owners here bought as an investment and have never lived in the property. A new flat can sit empty for months after handover, which is lost rent and a risk if nobody checks on it.

We help get new homes ready to rent: cleaning, utility connections, a move-in inventory and a verified first tenant.
