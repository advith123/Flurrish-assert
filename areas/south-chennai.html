In short

Flourrish manages houses and apartments in Adyar, Besant Nagar and Velachery in South Chennai. Many are older or inherited family homes whose owners now live abroad or in another city. Local knowledge

Why owners in South Chennai use a property manager.

Adyar and Besant Nagar are established neighbourhoods near the Adyar river and the beach, with many independent houses and older apartment blocks. Velachery is a busy residential and commercial hub with strong rental demand.

A lot of property here has been in the family for decades. Parents may have managed it until recently; now the children are abroad and the house is empty or rented informally.

Older buildings need more attention: plumbing, waterproofing before the monsoon, electrical wiring and termite checks. We plan this work with you instead of waiting for things to break.

Ask on WhatsApp
My parents' house in Adyar is empty now. Can you manage it?

Yes. We can look after it while it's empty, prepare it for renting, or both. Many of our South Chennai properties are family homes whose owners now live elsewhere.

Do older houses need more maintenance?

Usually, yes. We plan checks for plumbing, waterproofing, wiring and termites, and send you estimates before any work goes ahead.

Can you rent out separate portions of an independent house?

Yes, if each portion can be let on its own. Each tenant gets their own verification and agreement, and we report on each portion separately.

Do you check Velachery properties after heavy rain?

Yes. After heavy rain we check for water entry, drainage problems and damp, and send you photos.
