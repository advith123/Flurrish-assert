How to Manage Your Chennai Property From the USA

Owning a home in Chennai while living in the US is manageable once a few basics are in place:

  1. Appoint one trusted local manager as your single point of contact.
  2. Verify every tenant and keep a written, registered rent agreement.
  3. Have rent paid into the right Indian account and track it monthly.
  4. Schedule inspections and video walkthroughs so you see the property regularly.
  5. Put property tax, water, electricity and association dues on a calendar.
  6. Agree an approval limit for repairs so nothing major happens without you.

Banking, tax and repatriation rules depend on your situation. Confirm them with a qualified CA.

Tenant Verification Checklist in Tamil Nadu

  1. Government photo ID and current address proof.
  2. Proof of employment or income.
  3. Reference from a previous landlord.
  4. Police verification of the tenant.
  5. A written rent agreement, registered as required under Tamil Nadu's tenancy law.
  6. A move-in inventory with dated photos.

Procedures change from time to time. Check current requirements with a lawyer or the local authority.

NRI Tax Basics on Rental Income

  • Rent from a property in India is generally taxable in India, even if you live abroad.
  • Municipal taxes you pay and a standard deduction can reduce the taxable amount.
  • Tenants paying rent to an NRI are generally required to deduct tax at source (TDS).
  • You may need to file an Indian tax return, and tax treaties may give relief in your country of residence.

This is general information, not tax advice. Flourrish can coordinate with qualified legal, tax and banking professionals where required. Professional advice should be obtained from the appropriate licensed advisor.
