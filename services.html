Services

Everything your Chennai property needs, handled by one local team.

Fifteen services across tenants, property care and owner support. Use one, or hand over the lot.

In short

Flourrish offers 15 property management services in Chennai in three groups: tenants and rent (sourcing, police verification, agreements, rent collection, move-in and move-out), property care (inspections, video walkthroughs, maintenance, emergency response, vacant property care) and owner support (property tax, utility bills, association liaison, monthly reports and NRI coordination).

Tenants & rent

Finding the right people and making sure rent arrives.

  • Tenant sourcing

    Marketing your property, handling enquiries and running viewings.

  • Tenant verification

    Background checks and police verification before anyone moves in.

  • Rent agreements

    Written agreements prepared and registered with legal support.

  • Rent collection

    Due dates tracked, reminders sent and late payments followed up.

  • Move-in & move-out

    Inventory photos, key handover, deposit and utility coordination.

Property care

Keeping the property in good condition, and showing you proof.

  • Property inspections

    Regular visits with a documented condition report.

  • Video walkthroughs

    Room-by-room video so you can see the property yourself.

  • Maintenance coordination

    Vendor estimates, your approval, the repair and completion photos.

  • Emergency response

    Local response to leaks, faults and break-ins, with alerts to you.

  • Vacant property care

    Cleaning, leak and pest checks and security visits while it's empty.

Owner support

The paperwork and payments that come with owning property.

  • Property tax

    Authorised payments made on time, receipts kept with your documents.

  • Utility bills

    Electricity, water and gas paid on authorisation and reported monthly.

  • RWA & association liaison

    Association dues, notices and meetings handled for you.

  • Owner reports

    A monthly report covering rent, tenant, condition and expenses.

  • NRI support

    TDS, FEMA, POA and repatriation coordinated with CA and legal partners.
